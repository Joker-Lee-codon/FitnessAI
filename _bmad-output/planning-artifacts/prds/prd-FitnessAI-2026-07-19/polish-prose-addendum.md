| The initial application route uses the product owner's existing Apple Watch and iPhone. The Apple development-machine prerequisite is resolved. The first intended market is Apple Watch users in mainland China. | The initial application route uses the product owner's existing Apple Watch and iPhone. The prerequisite of having an Apple development machine has been met. The initial target users are Apple Watch users in mainland China. | Replaced the opaque “development-machine prerequisite is resolved” phrasing with an explicit statement and corrected the category mismatch between a market and a group of users. |
| FitnessAI remains within non-diagnostic fitness scope, never recommends rehabilitation exercises, and stops affected-scope planning when warning signs require qualified guidance or offline medical care. | FitnessAI remains within non-diagnostic fitness scope, never recommends rehabilitation exercises, and stops planning for the affected area when warning signs require guidance from a qualified professional or in-person medical care. | Clarified what “affected-scope planning” means and replaced the ambiguous phrase “offline medical care” with “in-person medical care.” |
| The rejected route is using Apple iCloud or CloudKit as the store for personal health information or account-scoped FitnessAI workout data. The accepted route separates local active-session authority from a compliant China-region application backend. | The rejected route is to use Apple iCloud or CloudKit to store personal health information or account-scoped FitnessAI workout data. The accepted route separates local authority over active sessions from a compliant China-region application backend. | Fixed the awkward route construction and unpacked the dense compound “local active-session authority” without changing the architecture boundary. |
| iPhone coordinates full correction, recovery, and transfer. | Consider: Does “full” describe the session record rather than the correction operation? If so, use “iPhone coordinates correction, recovery, and transfer of the complete session record.” | “Full correction” has more than one possible meaning. This query avoids imposing an interpretation while offering a precise revision if the intended object is the complete session record. |
| Start with simple context/history baselines and a compact feature model; consider a lightweight temporal model only if it adds stable held-out value. | Start with simple context/history baselines and a compact feature model; consider a lightweight temporal model only if it delivers a stable improvement on held-out data. | Replaced the undefined phrase “held-out value” with wording that states both the evaluation data and the required result. |
| The five-estimate window is a per-user interaction trust gate after a globally validated model exists; it is not scientific evidence that the model is release-ready and must not replace participant-grouped and temporal validation against simple baselines, uncertainty/risk–coverage analysis, missing-signal and subgroup reporting, physical-device resource tests, and acceptable prompt/correction burden. | The five-estimate window is a per-user interaction trust gate after a globally validated model exists; it is not scientific evidence that the model is release-ready and must not replace validation that groups data by participant, uses temporal splits, and compares results with simple baselines; uncertainty/risk–coverage analysis; missing-signal and subgroup reporting; physical-device resource tests; or measurement of prompt and correction burden. | Removed ambiguity about how the validation data is partitioned and used semicolons to expose the parallel release-evidence requirements. |
| Exact personalization, duration, missing-signal behavior, and cooldown rules require physical-device validation and false-positive/false-negative measurement. | The exact personalization method, duration thresholds, missing-signal behavior, and cooldown rules require physical-device validation and measurement of false-positive and false-negative rates. | Named the missing nouns behind “personalization” and “duration” and specified that the measured quantities are rates. |
