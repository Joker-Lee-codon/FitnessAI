- (event) Source fact: Formal sets stay quiet. User explicitly starts and finishes workouts; uncertain recognition and corrections occur during rest. A representative 20-set workout permits at most five proactive prompts across exercise, reps, load, and RPE, with p95 response at or below one second. Sources: PRD 35-43, 177-196, 312-315.
- (event) Source fact: The canonical record preserves Workout Session, Actual Set, Exercise, reps, Actual Load, optional RPE, timing, confidence, provenance, revision history, and Complete versus Incomplete states. Accepted, uncertain, corrected, unresolved, pending, and stale facts must remain visible and must never silently disappear or masquerade as confirmed. Sources: PRD 137-151, 177-196, 261-268.
- (event) Source fact: Recording is local-first and offline-first. Watch local durable commit is the saved boundary; phone and cloud synchronization are independent states and cannot block, roll back, duplicate, or overwrite a workout. Active session recovery must cover app termination, Watch restart, connectivity loss, sensor interruption, partial writes, delayed or out-of-order replay. Sources: PRD 233-241, 261-275; RPE research 148-218, 349-363.
- (event) Source fact: User correction is authoritative, reversible, scoped to the intended set, and auditable. Exercise corrections preserve other set fields; completed facts are revised rather than destructively overwritten; unsafe concurrent conflicts move to iPhone for explicit resolution. Sources: PRD FR-012 through FR-015, FR-046, FR-061 through FR-070.
- (event) Source fact: External load cannot be inferred reliably from wrist sensors. Plan or history may prefill it, but the user supplies or confirms the actual value. RPE is optional and confidence-gated; target, predicted, confirmed, auto-used, corrected, and effective RPE remain distinct, and low confidence causes abstention or pending rather than invisible insertion. Sources: Brief 30-36; PRD FR-016 through FR-027; RPE research 79-108, 204-218.
- (event) Source fact: The expected trust loop is automatic capture, fast correction during rest, optional post-workout review, safe save, and reuse of trustworthy history next time. Users tolerate occasional quickly correctable recognition errors but not missing or duplicate sets, destructive sync, expensive cleanup, or implied universal automatic coverage. Source: market research 123-129, 188-212, 229-265, 353-383.
- (event) Source fact: Consumer UX must treat motion, health, biometric, location, load, RPE, and workout history as sensitive. Permissions must be contextual and minimal; raw high-frequency HR and IMU remain local by default; export, deletion, consent, retention, account switching, and offline deletion propagation need explicit visible states. Health and RPE language is training assistance, not diagnosis or injury rehabilitation. Sources: PRD FR-042 through FR-053 and FR-067 through FR-070; market research 622-668; RPE research 125-133, 244-253, 873-890.
